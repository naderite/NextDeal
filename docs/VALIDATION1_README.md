## Key results for validation 1

- **Business:** 69 % of corporate clients hold a single product (1.47 on average); 320 individual contracts expire within one month (3.8 % unpaid); BH Assurance grew +4.5 % in 2025 vs +11.4 % for the market.
- **Data quality:** the prototype's recommendation score is **leaky** (`Bought` ≡ candidate == product already held → AUC 0.997 is meaningless). Rebuilt an honest leave-one-out target.
- **Preparation:** 178 + 66 missing cells → 0; hidden placeholders revealed; outliers treated with business rules; 440 episodes × 84 features.
- **Modeling (DSO1.1, reconstruction proxy):** client-grouped CV (3 × 5 folds). Logistic regression HitRate@3 = 0.686 vs 0.654 for popularity (+3.2 pts, company-bootstrap 95% CI +0.1 to +6.5); **long-tail HitRate@3 = 0.33 vs 0.11** (+22 pts, company-bootstrap 95% CI +15.8 to +29.6); coverage 17 vs 7 products.
- **Local tool demonstration (DSO1.3):** `recommend_products(client_id, k)` function, proposed tool schema + real output in `reports/tool_output_example.json`.

## Scope and proposed improvement

The inherited Next-BH prototype is a starting point, not an architecture or API constraint. The draft target uses a conversation orchestrator, cross-sell and renewal agents, model tools, advisor validation and monitoring. Guarantee recommendations (DSO2.2) belong to the renewal workflow; segmentation (DSO1.2) and customer value (DSO4.1) remain distinct objectives. Notebook 01 maps all twelve final DSOs to the target design.

The executed v1 benchmark reconstructs hidden owned products; it does not establish future purchases or campaign uplift. Only 34 of the 46 catalogue products have positive examples; the other 12 also lack branch mappings. Encoders are fitted within CV, but learned imputation mappings currently use the full company table; history-feature availability also needs auditing. Scores in the local tool are relative model scores, not calibrated purchase probabilities. Its automobile-to-automobile example exposes the need for suitability and diversification rules.

New v2 ranking, segmentation, guarantee-rule and NLP modules are implemented but not evaluated in the four notebooks. Integrated LLM calls, grounded drafts, deployment and monitoring are proposed. Renewal timing is a historical simulation of 1–27 September 2025. Targets of 0.70 overall and 0.40 long-tail remain goals, not achieved results.

Uncertainty is exported to `reports/model_gain_company_bootstrap.csv`: paired differences are averaged across CV repeats and bootstrapped by company (299 overall; 137 long-tail). The small overall interval narrowly excludes zero, conditional on preprocessing and model selection; no business-uplift claim follows.

The HTML deck includes two proposed-design slides: a dimensional warehouse model (snapshot facts, shared dimensions, contract-guarantee bridge and future event facts), and the ETL / retrieval pipeline separating current notebook outputs from planned engineering.
